From a global hub to flawless solutions.

Ten years of excellence · How a philosophy of global synergy reshaped the premium materials market.
I.

Hong Kong, 2016. A deliberate origin.

Our story began in 2016, in Hong Kong — a city where centuries of Eastern tradition meet the technological avant-garde of the West, and where global logistics operate with the precision of a chronometer.

To establish the House here was a strategic decision: not merely a distributor, but a global hub for architects, designers and manufactures of the luxury segment. A single coordinate from which authentic materials would reach the world.

II.

A simple, ambitious mission.

To unite within a single portfolio materials usually divided by thousands of kilometres and complex chains of supply. The author of a premium interior should find in one place everything: relict Kauri timber, aerospace steel, monolithic basalt.

Convenience, here, is not a luxury — it is the foundation on which precision and time-to-market are built.

III.

A decade of evolution.

Over ten years we have evolved from a local expert into a reliable international B2B partner. Years were invested in establishing direct contracts with unique marble quarries, licensed suppliers of authentic slabs, and leading laboratories of high-optic polymer.

In parallel, we launched our own high-technology production of architectural metals and hardware — to control, with absolute autonomy, every millimetre of jointing in the final object.

IV.

Today: a guarantor.

Today our Hong Kong office stands as a guarantor of financial stability, of reinforced legal engineering and of impeccable Quality Control for clients worldwide.

We do not merely supply materials. We supply the certainty that each singular render of an architect will be materialised — exactly on time, and in flawless condition.
